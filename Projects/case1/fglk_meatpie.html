Grandma Lukenoff's Piroshki

by Anna-Lena

A traditional Russian stuffed bun or pastry, using yeast dough that is filled with ground meet, eggs and seasonings, baked or pan fry. Grandman and I prefer frying on stove top. (12 servings).

Ingredients

  • 1 cake or dry yeast
  • 2 cups lukewarm scaleded milk
  • 2 cups lukewarm water
  • 1/2 cup sugar
  • 1/2 cup oil
  • 3 small or 2 large eggs
  • 1 tablespoon salt
  • 7 cups flour unbleached
  • 6 hard boiled eggs chopped
  • 3 lbs ground meat
  • chopped onion
  • chopped cabbage
  • any of your favirote seasoning

Directions

  1. Preheat oven to 350° F (180° C).
  2. Dissolve the yeast in 2 cups of lukewarm water
  3. Add 2 cups of lukewarm scalded milk mix throughly
  4. Add 1 TBSP salt
  5. 1/2 cup sugar
  6. 2 lrg or 3 sm eggs
  7. 1/2 cup oil mix throughly
  8. Add 7 cups flour
  9. Cover and let rise for 6-8 hours
  10. 1 hour before dough is done rising prepare the filling
  11. Saute the onions and cabbage
  12. Add ground meat
  13. Add sseasonings and cook fully
  14. Add chopped hard boiled eggs
  15. Let filling cool for 30 mins
  16. Once dough is ready if frying you can fill the dough and fry as you stuff
  17. Dough balls should be about the size of a golf ball, then flatten by hand
  18. Add filling and then fold the sides and pinch closed
  19. Then bake or fry, if baking make as many that will fit in your pan of choice
